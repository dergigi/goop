Goop’s first release: a fork of [Coop](https://github.com/lumehq/coop), with a focus on everyday usability and conversations with AI agents over Nostr.

### What’s new

- Markdown in chat messages: bold, italic, strikethrough, headings, lists, links, and shaded monospace code. Toggle it in **Settings → Appearance → Render Markdown**.
- Preserved line breaks and indentation, plus corrected mention and link offsets after media extraction.
- Media uploads use your account’s published Blossom servers in preference order, with your logged-in signer for authorization. The local server setting (default: `blossom.band`) remains the fallback when no usable published list is available.
- **⌘,** on macOS and **Ctrl+,** on Windows/Linux open Settings.
- Contacts and Results sections now collapse and expand.
- Startup indicators distinguish identity restoration from conversation loading.
- A clear button replaces the search icon when the field contains text and cancels pending searches.
- Goop branding, purple accents, app icons, and fallback avatars.

### Downloads

Choose the installer for your operating system and CPU: **arm64** for Apple Silicon/ARM, **x64** for Intel/AMD. `SHA256SUMS` contains checksums for all downloads.

macOS and Windows installers are not signed with a developer certificate; macOS builds are not notarized. Your operating system may require explicit approval to open them. Linux Snap packages are unsigned local packages and require `snap install --dangerous --classic <file.snap>`.

Goop uses its own application identity and can be installed alongside Coop. Thanks to Coop’s contributors for the foundation.
